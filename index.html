01 · Manifesto

Good ideas should leave the page.

We shape them through design, engineering, and hands-on work.

We learn by making, testing, and using what we build.

Rooted in Mexico, made to go further.

  • Design with intent

    Every geometry, material and process has a reason. Nothing is added just to look good.

  • Make it real

    An idea isn’t finished until it’s physical. We prototype early, and what leaves the shop is finished properly.

  • Prove it in use

    We use what we make before we offer it. No part leaves without time on the bike.

  • Go further

    We try new processes and take things where they’re used. One new technique per project.

03 · Process

How we work

Every part goes through the same five stages. You always know where your project is and what comes next.

  1. Requirements

    We talk through the problem, how the part is used and where it goes.

  2. Design

    Sketches and a 3D model to review together before cutting any material.

  3. Prototype

    A first real part to measure, fit and adjust.

  4. Test

    We take it out on the trail. Whatever fails gets fixed here.

  5. Production

    We build the final version, with drawings and a part number.

04 · The workshop

From Ocotepec to Monterrey

Ocote is the resin-rich pine splinter used to start a fire in the mountains: small, resourceful and reliable when it counts. That’s where the name comes from, along with Ocotepec, the town where it all began.

OcoteWorks is run by an industrial designer with an engineer’s mind, shaped by product engineering and the craft of mosaic. We work from Monterrey, with the Cerro de la Silla on the horizon.

Engineering
Drawings, tolerances and testing. Every part has a number and a revision.
Design
Form, color and use, worked out together.
Craft
Mosaic taught us to build piece by piece.
Adventure
We make what we’d take to the mountains ourselves.
