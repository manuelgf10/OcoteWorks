01Data controller

Manuel García Flores, an individual operating under the trade name OcoteWorks, is responsible for the processing of your personal data.

Address: Monterrey, Nuevo León, Mexico. For anything related to your data, contact hola@ocoteworks.com. The same address handles ARCO rights requests.

02Data we collect

Only what you give us:

  • Quote builder: name, email, project or brand name, anything you write in the notes and the project configuration you chose.
  • Product waitlists: email and, where applicable, the finish and number of pieces you’re interested in.
  • Direct email: whatever you include in your message.

We do not collect sensitive personal data. Please don’t include it in your messages.

03What we use it for

Purposes needed to handle what you ask for:

  • Reply to your request, ask questions about the project and send you a proposal and quote.
  • Follow up on the project if you decide to go ahead.
  • Let you know when a waitlisted product is available and gauge interest in products in pre-production, to decide what to make.

We don’t use your data for advertising, we don’t sell it and we don’t share it with third parties for their own purposes. If we ever wanted to use it for something else, we would ask for your consent first.

04How to limit its use

You can ask us at any time to stop contacting you, to remove you from the waitlist or to delete your information. Just write to hola@ocoteworks.com from the email you used to contact us.

05ARCO rights and withdrawal

You have the right to access, rectify and cancel your data or object to its use (ARCO rights), and to withdraw your consent. To exercise them, write to hola@ocoteworks.com with:

  • Your name and the email you used to contact us, which identifies you.
  • The right you want to exercise and a clear description of the data involved.

We will reply through the same channel within the time limits set by Mexico’s Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

If you believe your rights were not respected, you may contact the competent data protection authority, currently Mexico’s Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno.

06Service providers

To run the site we use services that process data on our behalf and only to provide their service:

  • Formspree (United States): receives the site’s forms and forwards them to us by email.
  • Google / Gmail: the inbox where we receive and answer messages.
  • GoatCounter: cookie-free visit statistics. It records the page visited, where you came from, browser type, screen size and approximate country and region; it doesn’t store your IP address or identify you.
  • GitHub Pages (hosting), Adobe Fonts and Google Fonts (typefaces): when pages load they may receive technical data from your browser, such as your IP address, under their own privacy notices.

We do not transfer your data to third parties.

07Cookies and similar technologies

The site uses no cookies and no advertising tools. To know how many people visit, we use GoatCounter, which counts visits and a few actions (such as pressing “Buy” or sending a form) in aggregate and without cookies. The site also stores a flag in your browser during your visit so the loading animation plays once; it doesn’t identify you and is cleared when you close the tab.

08Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed to handle your request and follow up on the project. Waitlist emails are kept until the product is available or until you ask to be removed.

09Changes to this notice

Any change will be published on this page with its update date.
