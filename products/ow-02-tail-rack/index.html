04 · Installation

Four bolts

It mounts on the four points Yamaha left on the T7 for a rack. No cutting, no drilling.

  • IncludesOW-02 rack + 4 new bolts
  • ToolsAllen keys
  • Preliminary guide
  1. 01

    Prep the bike

    On flat, stable ground, switched off, with the engine cold.

  2. 02

    Remove the factory bolts

    With an Allen key, remove the 4 bolts from the mounting points Yamaha left for a rack: two on each side. Keep them in case you ever take the rack off.

  3. 03

    Offer up the rack

    Set it on the tail and line up its 4 holes with the mounting points.

  4. 04

    Fit the new bolts

    Thread the 4 kit bolts in by hand, without tightening, so the rack settles on its own.

  1. 05

    Tighten in a cross pattern

    Follow the 1 → 4 order in the drawing. Tighten firmly and check there’s no play.

  2. 06

    Check at 100 km

    Re-check the bolts after the first 100 km and every so often, especially after dirt roads.

Preliminary guide. Torque values and final details will be published with the first batch.
