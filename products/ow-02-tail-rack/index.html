01 · Design

Just enough, nothing more

The idea: a rack you barely notice on the bike, but that’s there when you need to strap something down.

01

One piece

6061 aluminum sheet, laser cut and folded. No welds, no joints.

02

Low profile

Follows the line of the seat and tail. From a distance the bike looks stock.

03

Tie-down points

Slots for straps and small bags, with CNC-rounded edges so they won’t cut webbing.
